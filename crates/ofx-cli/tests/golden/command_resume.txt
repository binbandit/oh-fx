oh-fx resume

Continue a saved interactive session

Usage:
  oh-fx session resume [last|<id>] | session resume --id <id> | --resume [last|<id>] | resume [last|<id>] | resume --id <id> | --resume-last | --continue | -c | -r | --resume-<id>

Options:
  -r         Choose the session to resume from a picker
  last       Resume the most recent session
  <id>       Resume a session by id
  --id <id>  Resume a session by exact id
